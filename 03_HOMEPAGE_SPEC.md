# 03 --- Homepage Specification

## Homepage role

The homepage is not a catalogue.

It should create a narrative:

**Curiosity → Culture → People → Experience → Impact → Participation**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 01 --- Hero

## Goal

Make the visitor immediately understand that Kiiro connects cultural
heritage with hands-on experiences.

## Visual

Preferred: - real workshop footage - real artisan process - hands
working - materials - movement - place

Avoid: - generic stock meditation - AI-generated "Indian village"
scenes - generic cultural collage - excessive overlays

## Content

Small eyebrow: `Where Heritage Meets Hands-On Learning`

Primary heading: A concise brand statement.

Supporting copy: 1--2 lines maximum.

CTAs: - Explore Experiences - View Weekly Events

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 02 --- Manifesto + Impact

Introduce the philosophy.

Use a strong editorial statement followed by the impact statistics.

Example structure:

``` text
We believe culture is not something to preserve behind glass.
It is something to learn, make, share and carry forward.

800+ Workshops
10,000+ Consumers
7 Cities
15+ Corporates
10+ Resorts
```

The exact copy must be client-approved.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 03 --- Artforms

Three visual chapters:

### Traditional

Living traditions and heritage practices.

### Contemporary

New interpretations and collaborations.

### Wellness

Creative practices that support mindful participation and connection.

Use editorial rows rather than identical cards.

Each artform item: - image - title - region - short description - link

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 04 --- Experiences by Audience

Four routes:

-   Schools & Colleges
-   Corporates
-   Hospitality & Luxury
-   Online Experiences

Each should answer:

**Who is this for?** **What happens?** **Why Kiiro?**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 05 --- Artisan Impact

This section must make the human side visible.

Show: - artisan portrait - hands - materials - teaching - quote - impact
story

Possible structure:

``` text
The craft is the experience.
The artisan is the knowledge.

[artisan image]

Name
Craft
Region

Quote

[Read the artisan story]
```

Do not fabricate the quote.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 06 --- Partnerships & CSR

This section should be visually distinct.

Explain that Kiiro can design structured cultural programs around:

-   capacity building
-   artisan development
-   community engagement
-   gifting
-   custom needs

CTA: `Build a Partnership`

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 07 --- Journal

Show three editorial stories.

Prioritize: - craft knowledge - artisan stories - cultural context -
process - event stories - community - responsible contemporary
interpretations

Do not publish generic SEO filler.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 08 --- Enquiry

Split the intent:

### Corporate / Institutional

`Plan a Program`

### Individual / Experience

`Find an Experience`

The form experience should adapt based on the selected intent.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 09 --- Social

Use curated social content.

Do not make the homepage dependent on a third-party social embed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 10 --- Footer

Include:

-   navigation
-   contact
-   social
-   WhatsApp
-   newsletter
-   cities/locations
-   legal
-   copyright

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Homepage motion

Motion should reinforce the material nature of the brand.

Examples: - slow image reveal - crop/uncrop transitions - subtle
horizontal movement - natural parallax - type entering as the user
reaches a section

Avoid: - constant floating elements - random blobs - excessive
scroll-jacking - kinetic typography everywhere - animation on every card

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Homepage content source

The homepage must be driven by a Sanity `homePage` singleton.

Do not hard-code: - impact statistics - featured artforms - featured
experiences - testimonials - partners - journal items - social posts
