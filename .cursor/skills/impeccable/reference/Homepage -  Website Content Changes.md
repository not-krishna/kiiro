# **6\. NEW SECTION: "Why making restores"**

**ADD**  Place directly after "How a session unfolds" and before "Upcoming Events". A simple 5-card layout works.

**Headline:** Why a creative break works better

**Intro:** A scroll isn't rest, and neither is another meeting. Making things by hand offers a different kind of break.

**Cards (title \+ text):**

1\.       **Calms the nervous system:** Rhythmic, repetitive motions like stamping, weaving, or shading help your body shift out of stress mode. Many people notice slower breathing and a softer mind within minutes.

2\.       **Quiets overthinking:** When you're absorbed in colour, texture, and process, there's no room left for the inner critic. This state of flow is often described as meditative.

3\.       **Lifts your mood:** Finishing something with your own hands brings a real sense of accomplishment, a small, earned feeling of "I made this."

4\.       **Gives feelings a form:** Some things are hard to say. Making gives emotions a place to go, without needing words.

5\.       **Builds belonging:** Working side by side helps ease loneliness. Shared making builds connection faster than small talk ever does.

**Small print below the cards:** \*Research on creative activity and wellbeing is encouraging. Kiiro sessions support relaxation and wellbeing and are not a substitute for medical or mental health care.\*
