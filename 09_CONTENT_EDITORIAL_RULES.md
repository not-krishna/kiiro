# 09 --- Cultural Editorial & Anti-AI-Slop Rules

## Why this exists

The biggest risk in this project is not technical.

It is cultural flattening.

A technically excellent website can still fail if it looks like an
AI-generated collection of "Indian culture" stereotypes.

This document is mandatory for both human designers and coding agents.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 01 --- Real before generated

When real Kiiro photography exists, use it.

Priority:

``` text
Real artisan
↓
Real workshop
↓
Real object/process
↓
Approved archival material
↓
Approved illustration
↓
AI-generated conceptual visual
```

AI-generated imagery must never be used to represent: - a real artisan -
a real historical scene - documentary evidence - an actual workshop - a
real cultural object - an actual client event

unless explicitly labelled as conceptual.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 02 --- Don't genericise India

Never create visuals or copy that imply:

"India = orange + temple + sari + mandala + elephant."

Indian craft traditions are regional, specific, living practices.

Whenever possible, show: - the specific craft - the region - the
material - the process - the artisan - the community context

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 03 --- The hand is important

The brief explicitly values hands at work.

Use process imagery: - shaping clay - stamping blocks - stitching -
painting - dyeing - carving - weaving - preparing materials

These images communicate knowledge transfer better than generic smiling
portraits.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 04 --- Avoid fake imperfection

Do not add: - fake scratches - fake paper grain - fake ink marks - fake
hand-drawn arrows - random brush strokes

unless they are part of an approved brand asset system.

Authenticity should come from the content.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 05 --- Avoid cultural decoration without meaning

Do not put: - mandalas - rangoli - generic tribal patterns - paisley -
Devanagari - temple geometry - random folk illustrations

around the site merely to signal "Indian."

A visual element must have: - cultural relevance - brand relevance -
compositional purpose

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 06 --- Copywriting rules

Prefer:

> Learn a technique directly from the people who keep it alive.

Avoid:

> Embark on a soulful journey into the timeless tapestry of India's rich
> heritage.

Prefer:

> Spend an afternoon working with clay, guided by an artisan.

Avoid:

> Unlock your inner artist through a transformative experience.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 07 --- Don't overclaim wellness

Kiiro may communicate creative wellbeing.

Do not turn this into medical claims.

Avoid: - cures anxiety - treats depression - clinically proven healing -
therapy claims - guaranteed stress reduction

unless the client provides appropriate evidence and legal approval.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 08 --- Artisan representation

An artisan should never be treated as decorative imagery.

Where appropriate, include: - name - craft - region - practice - story -
teaching role - quote

The website should help visitors understand the person behind the
object.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 09 --- Cultural context

Artform pages should answer:

-   What is this?
-   Where is it practiced?
-   Who practices it?
-   What materials are involved?
-   What techniques are used?
-   How is knowledge transmitted?
-   What does it mean in its context?
-   How is it evolving?
-   How does Kiiro engage with it?

Only answer what is verified.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 10 --- Illustration rules

Illustration can be used for: - conceptual editorial moments - section
dividers - learning diagrams - abstract storytelling

It should not replace real documentary imagery where authenticity
matters.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 11 --- Visual rhythm

Not every section should have: - rounded image - title - description -
button

Use: - full bleed - split layouts - editorial captions - image essays -
pull quotes - large type - small metadata - process sequences

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 12 --- AI coding agent rule

If an AI coding agent proposes:

"Let's add a subtle Indian-inspired pattern/background to make it feel
cultural."

Reject it unless there is a specific approved asset/reference.

The cultural identity comes from **content and art direction**, not
decoration.
